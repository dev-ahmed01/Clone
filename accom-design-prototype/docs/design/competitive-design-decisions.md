# Competitive Design Decisions

This matrix combines the supplied Amber audit, public-page observation and research hypotheses. It does not claim measured competitor performance.

| Decision | Pattern | User problem | Evidence / hypothesis | Design response | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KEEP | Rich property and room photography | Remote students need spatial confidence. | App reviews; Reddit photo concerns; brief. | Immersive category-labelled gallery plus room-specific illustrative media disclosure. | P1 |
| KEEP | Room types and tenancy choices | Students should compare without losing property context. | StudentCrowd pattern; persona tasks. | Compact expandable rows with tenure and total. | P0 |
| KEEP | University location context | Distance affects daily life and budget. | Operator facts; student-platform patterns. | Campus-first commute panel with source and mode. | P1 |
| KEEP | Detailed amenities and inclusions | Students need to know what rent covers. | Operator policy; Persona A/D. | Separate included, paid and unverified items. | P0 |
| KEEP | Important policy access | Conditions can carry material financial risk. | App complaints; Persona A/D. | Plain-language summaries before enquiry. | P0 |
| KEEP | Persistent enquiry action | Students need a clear route to help while comparing. | Brief; Accom model. | Sticky room-aware CTA on desktop and mobile. | P1 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Long simultaneous section navigation | Too many labels compete with the room task. | Local Amber audit; cognitive-load hypothesis. | Six short anchors in a horizontally scrollable glass rail. | P1 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Repeated promotions and trust badges | Repetition displaces decision facts without adding proof. | Brief; complaint risk. | No offer component because no offer is verified; one dated provenance note. | P1 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Competing “Book” and “Enquire” CTAs | Users may mistake assistance for confirmation. | Persona tasks; Reddit legitimacy concern. | One “Enquire” verb and repeated non-booking status. | P0 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Nested card stacks | Equal containers erase hierarchy and increase scroll. | Local audit and design hypothesis. | Open sections/rules; cards only around comparison decisions. | P2 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Full policy text by default | Dense text is unlikely to be read at the decision point. | Brief and travel policy patterns. | Accordions with honest summaries and need for full terms. | P1 |
| REMOVE / REDUCE | Long nearby-place lists | Volume does not answer campus convenience. | Persona B hypothesis. | Two verified university destinations plus Central London context. | P2 |
| REDESIGN | Headline weekly price | A low weekly price hides total commitment. | App tenancy complaint; Persona A/D; Baymard transfer. | Selected tenure shows weekly, dates, weeks and total together. | P0 |
| REDESIGN | Room selection/comparison | Switching cards taxes working memory. | Persona tasks; Student Roost case-study claim. | Select up to two and compare in one table. | P1 |
| REDESIGN | Availability | “Available” can become stale during a booking process. | Firsthand app report. | “Request availability” plus dated demo provenance. | P0 |
| REDESIGN | Reviews | Unexplained ratings blur platform and property evidence. | StudentCrowd/Booking review patterns. | Omit unauthorised score; document the provenance requirement. | P1 |
| REDESIGN | Mobile CTA | Fixed controls can obscure content or become hard to reach. | WCAG 2.2; local Amber audit; Persona B/E. | Safe-area bottom pill, page/scroll padding and 44 px+ targets. | P0 |
| REDESIGN | Enquiry modal | Login, status and support ambiguity cause anxiety. | App/Reddit reports; brief. | Minimal labelled form, inline errors, pending success and no payment. | P0 |
| ADD | At-a-glance summary | Students need a fast fit check. | All personas. | Three verified decision facts before the room list. | P1 |
| ADD | Media provenance | Photos may be mistaken for exact rooms. | Reddit threads. | “Illustrative media” on gallery, room rows and lightbox. | P0 |
| ADD | Access enquiry route | A generic badge cannot establish suitability. | Persona E; WCAG. | Explicit unknowns and accessibility topic preselection. | P0 |
| ADD | Honest timestamp and source | Dynamic facts need context. | Availability evidence; brief. | Checked date in overview and provenance in data. | P1 |
