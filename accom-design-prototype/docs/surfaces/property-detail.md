# Property Detail Surface Brief

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A property page should feel like a well-edited move-in field guide: immersive enough to imagine living there, precise enough to make a rental decision. It refuses the category default of a promotional hero followed by repetitive equal-weight cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm paper and ivory grounds, midnight ink, Accom red as the action signal, cobalt and mint as supporting annotations, editorial Bricolage headlines and compact Montserrat labels. Rules, open sections, numbered comparison cells and occasional glass controls recall a carefully assembled student move-in dossier without pretending to be a legal document.

**STORY:** A student first sees the actual kind of place being considered, then understands fit and verified location, compares room/tenancy trade-offs, checks what is included and what remains unknown, and finally sends a clearly non-binding, room-specific enquiry.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A restrained global header sits above a full-bleed, asymmetric four-image gallery. Property identity and the starting decision context overlap the lower gallery edge. On desktop the first room/action context is visible without turning the opening into a generic split hero; on mobile a compact bottom enquiry bar is present from the start.

**FORM:** Student move-in field guide, candidate 4 of 7 grounded directions (campus welcome zine, transit wayfinding, university prospectus, move-in field guide, riverside atlas, student-union poster wall, lease blueprint). `seed:aebf6b6cdd297e6b`. Signature interaction: selecting a tenancy updates total commitment in both the room row and sticky enquiry surface without losing context.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
